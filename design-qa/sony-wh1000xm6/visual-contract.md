# WH-1000XM6 visual contract

- **Surface and job:** The selected-headset workbench; check live headphone state and make the routine sound changes that otherwise require Sony Sound Connect.
- **Visual authority:** The current Switchboard device workbench, `DESIGN.md`, and the user brief. Sony's hardware presentation informs product prominence, not the application's chrome.
- **First viewport:** The exact product render and battery lead; Noise Control and the complete ten-band EQ remain visible at 1080 x 720. Sound-processing choices follow without turning into a card grid.
- **Hierarchy and density:** Product identity first, ANC and EQ second, processing and local presets third, uncommon metadata in Diagnostics.
- **Type roles:** Existing compact Switchboard display, section, label, body, and tabular numeric roles.
- **Color and material:** Existing ink and graphite surfaces, steel dividers, Signal Blue only for interaction, and the EQ band palette for graph nodes. No gradients, glow, glass, or decorative shadows.
- **Control grammar:** Exposed segmented choices for modes and presets, the existing switch primitive for booleans, a horizontal slider for ambient level, and a keyboard-operable graphical EQ.
- **Signature:** A large, color-accurate XM6 render beside a continuous sound console, with the EQ curve visually aligned to its ten physical band controls.
- **Anti-reference:** A generic settings dashboard made from equal-weight rounded cards or a developer-oriented Bluetooth inspector.
- **Critical states:** Connecting, connected, disconnected, control-channel busy, pending write, rejected write, unknown color, selected color, and partial capability support.
- **Responsive constraints:** No page-level horizontal overflow at 1080 x 720, 1420 x 900, or 1920 x 1080; the 720 px composition keeps battery, Noise Control, and EQ reachable without mandatory scrolling.
