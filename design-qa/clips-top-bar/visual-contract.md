# Clips top bar visual contract

## Reference hierarchy

- A quiet recorder rail spans the top of the Capture workspace and keeps status, replay enablement, capture settings, shortcut, and source in one readable line.
- The library row places the Clips identity and Create Montage together on the left, with bounded search, filters, sort, and view controls aligned to the right.
- Date groups use label and count only. Long horizontal separators do not divide every section.

## Geometry and behavior

- The recorder rail remains usable without horizontal page overflow at 1080 x 720, 1420 x 900, and 1920 x 1080.
- Search is intentionally bounded and yields space before hiding secondary labels at narrow widths.
- Existing replay settings, direct enable switch, source picker, library filters, sort, view, and montage actions remain connected to canonical state.
- Focus visibility, keyboard order, reduced motion, notice states, and sticky positioning remain intact.

## Visual language

- Use spacing, typography, and restrained tonal surfaces for hierarchy.
- Borders belong to interactive controls and popovers, not as repeated full-width page dividers.
- Keep the existing dark Switchboard palette and compact desktop density. No gradients, glow, glass, or decorative motion.
