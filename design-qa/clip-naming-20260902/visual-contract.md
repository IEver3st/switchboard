# Clip naming visual contract

- Surface and job: Capture library; distinguish and find saved clips quickly.
- Visual authority: the supplied SteelSeries Moments library for hierarchy, the supplied Switchboard screenshot for the defect, and `DESIGN.md` for product character.
- First viewport: thumbnails remain dominant; readable identity and capture time sit directly below each thumbnail.
- Hierarchy and density: clip title first, game and capture provenance second, relative age third; keep the existing compact five-column library.
- Type roles: sentence-case title, uppercase metadata, tabular time values.
- Color and material: retain Switchboard's neutral continuous surface and existing semantic tokens.
- Control grammar: preserve current card, favorite, share, overflow, selection, and context-menu behavior.
- Signature: generated names use `Switchboard Capture · date, time, and local time zone`; date groups repeat the calendar date for fast scanning.
- Anti-reference: a wall of identical `Display 1 clip` or `Desktop clip` labels with no absolute date context.
- Critical states: generated, renamed, auto-captured, grid, list, empty, selection, overflow, and narrow desktop.
- Responsive constraints: no horizontal overflow at 1080x720, 1420x900, or 1920x1080; titles may truncate but time identity must remain discoverable.
