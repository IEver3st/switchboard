# Switchboard 0.9.5

G502 X Plus lighting now restores the last accepted selection at startup even
when the previous lighting status became Unknown. Switchboard also reapplies
that selection when the mouse changes RGB power or ownership while the app is
running, using the existing five-second device check.

- Keeps Off, effect, color, brightness, speed, direction, and zone colors across
  restoration. A failed check no longer discards the saved selection.
- Preserves battery warnings, battery cutoff, and status cues during recovery,
  then restores the selected lighting when those temporary effects clear.
- Retries failed lighting discovery instead of leaving the session permanently
  without lighting controls.
- Leaves healthy animations running without repeatedly restarting them. No
  additional polling timer or onboard profile writes are introduced.

Regression coverage includes failed readback, receiver state loss, repeated
session restarts, legacy saved settings, and temporary lighting overrides. On
the connected mouse, the saved Off choice was restored from Unknown; RGB power
readback remained Off across two subsequent checks after the diagnostic handle
closed. Physical sleep/wake behavior and a long-running hardware soak remain
unverified.
