Switchboard 0.9.26 improves microphone cleanup after speech and fixes microphone test playback.

- RNNoise now uses speech confidence to fade residual background noise and late room reflections after speech, while preserving short pauses and the selected suppression strength. It adds no processing delay.
- With monitoring disabled, Test microphone plays through the current main output instead of an old monitoring destination. Enabled monitoring keeps its selected output.

Validation: Audio.Host regression tests and three live QuadCast 2 capture start/stop cycles passed with no capture overruns or bypassed frames. Offline speech and reflection comparisons showed reduced late room tails with minimal overall speech-level change. These checks do not establish voice quality in a live Discord call or removal of reflections during speech. The Windows installer remains unsigned.
