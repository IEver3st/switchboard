Switchboard 0.9.1 fixes Quick controls access and clip trimming.

- Register the Quick controls shortcut in the main app so it remains available when Switchboard is unfocused or in the tray. Press once to open and again to close.
- Choose and enable the shortcut from the panel's App tab, with feedback when another app has reserved it.
- Replace native dropdowns with styled, keyboard-accessible menus and refine panel spacing, focus, and narrow-window layouts.
- Fix replay saves from Quick controls being rejected as untrusted IPC.
- Keep the full source timeline visible while trimming a single clip, and prevent the preview from flashing “Preparing montage” during trim adjustments.

Validation includes hidden native Electron panel checks, shortcut registration and lifecycle checks, persisted settings, clip-trim regressions, and deterministic JavaScript and native-host suites. Physical shortcut input in foreground games and installed-update acceptance were not revalidated. The Windows installer is unsigned unless signing credentials are configured.
