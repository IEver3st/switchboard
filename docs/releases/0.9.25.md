- Game, Chat, and Media each have their own spatial stage, with shared head tracking and optional managed OpenTrack setup.
- Replay can record Game and Media together, Chat separately, and the processed microphone on its own track. Explicit device selections remain selected through disconnections.

- Microphone monitoring sits beside the channel controls, with clearer unavailable and unconfirmed states.
- Audio playback queues recover from excess backlog while recording queues preserve continuity. Microphone processing accepts partial packets when noise suppression is bypassed.
- Startup feedback, G502 lighting behavior, and duplicate clip handling are refined. Previous shortcut, clip-library, onboarding memory, and updater fixes are retained.
