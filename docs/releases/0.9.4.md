# Switchboard 0.9.4

Fixes a recording bug that could add crackling to saved clips and replays, including recordings from SteelSeries Sonar. Capture now preserves continuous audio samples instead of inserting silence or cutting samples when packet timestamps fluctuate. Real missing-packet gaps and audio/video startup alignment are preserved.

This fix applies to new recordings. It cannot restore audio already damaged in saved clips.

Validation: the regression reproduced sample corruption before the fix and preserved identical PCM after it. A 15-second live timing probe on the Sonar system input changed from 2,181 inserted frames and 2,164 cut frames to zero. The selected microphone also showed zero inserted or cut frames. Replay synchronization remained within 22 ms, and three live capture start/stop cycles completed without dropped packets.

Listening confirmation on the installed update and long-running hardware validation remain separate from these checks.
