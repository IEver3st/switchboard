Switchboard 0.9.28 fixes overlapping application routing and adds clear recovery actions when Audio cannot deliver sound.

- Apps with multiple processes now share their existing capture owner. Moving a child process between Game, Chat and Media moves the owning capture, and selecting a parent replaces overlapping child captures.
- A compact warning beneath the Audio tabs explains stopped routing, disconnected outputs, Windows mute or zero volume, and a muted Personal mix. Its action opens the relevant settings, restarts Audio, or restores the mix.
- Restarting Audio releases existing routes before reopening the saved configuration. Windows default audio devices and saved mix settings are preserved.
- Windows endpoint mute and volume readback now inform the warning, and unavailable routing is reflected in the Audio status.

Validation includes JavaScript and native audio tests, native Electron checks at all three supported window sizes, and live Windows playback readback for Game, Chat and Media on WH-1000XM6 headphones. Live routing checks also cover process-tree moves, repeated starts, killed-host recovery and restoration of application output roles. These checks do not establish listening quality or confirm every application's behavior in a normal session.
