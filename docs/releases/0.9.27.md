Switchboard 0.9.27 rebuilds all ten microphone presets to preserve voice body and reduce excessive processing.

- Deep Voice follows the warmth and broad midrange dip of a Sonar reference curve, with restrained highs. This is a tonal approximation, not a copy of ClearCast processing.
- Natural Voice keeps the speech range nearly neutral. Studio provides a flat reference with gating, suppression and compression off, while retaining peak protection.
- Clear Speech, Warm & Smooth, Podcast, Broadcast, Crisp, Streamer and Noisy Room each have revised EQ, compression and cleanup settings for their intended use.
- Existing saved sound, custom presets and monitoring preferences are preserved. After updating, reselect a factory microphone preset to apply its new tuning.

Validation includes native DSP response and peak checks, offline quiet-speech fixtures, persistence tests and hidden Electron preset checks at all supported window sizes. Listening quality still depends on the microphone, voice, room and downstream processing.
