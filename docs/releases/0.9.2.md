# Switchboard 0.9.2

Size-limited clip exports now choose a resolution that better fits the available video bitrate. This reduces compression artifacts when sharing moving game footage under a 10, 25, or 50 MB limit. Edited clips and montages use the same resolution policy, including portrait exports.

Original exports retain their existing resolution behavior. Longer clips still benefit from a larger size limit.

Validation covered real FFmpeg exports, file-size limits, duration, frame rate, portrait framing, and Original resolution. A 31-second War Thunder clip exported under 10 MB at 720p with improved measured quality compared with the previous 1440p output.

The Windows installer is unsigned. Installed-update execution and hardware-encoder export acceptance were not rerun for this patch.
