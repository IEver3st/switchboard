Switchboard 0.9.15 adds microphone timing calibration for saved clips.

- Open Settings → Capture → Audio → Microphone timing to measure delay through your headphones and microphone.
- Hold one headphone earcup near the microphone during the eleven-second test, then save the measured correction. Future clips automatically move the microphone earlier on the matching device pair.
- Fine-tune the correction or reset it. Failed or cancelled tests preserve your saved setting.
- Saving a correction restarts the replay buffer. Existing clips are unchanged.

Calibration supports output-device system audio. Recalibrate after changing headphones, Bluetooth mode, or Sonar routing or processing. It compensates recorded voice timing; it does not reduce live Bluetooth latency.

Synthetic media and native UI checks passed. Acoustic accuracy with individual Sonar/Bluetooth setups still requires a calibration and listening check.
