# Switchboard 0.9.8

- Capture automatically retries recorder failures with increasing delays while
  enabled, preserving the requested source and encoder until recovery succeeds.
- Replay status and duration share one settings control. Clips being saved now
  appear as pending items in grid and list views.
- Increasing replay length keeps the recorder running and allows its buffer to
  grow to the new duration. Replay storage estimates handle high bitrates.
- Setup saves capture and audio choices before starting replay once on Finish.
  Failed starts keep setup open for retry.
- Windows audio devices remain discoverable when their drivers omit optional
  metadata.
- Edit and montage drafts expire three hours after their last save. Original
  clips and imported audio remain untouched.
- Razer, HyperX, and Logitech modules start disabled on new profiles. Existing
  module choices are preserved.

The Windows installer is unsigned. Live capture recovery, physical-device
behavior, installed-app updating, and long-running soak behavior require
separate validation.
