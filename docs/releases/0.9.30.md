Switchboard 0.9.30 rebuilds microphone noise removal to preserve more voice while cleaning up background sound.

- Removed the extra speech attenuation layer that could cut quiet syllables and word endings. Speech detection now protects the original voice contribution when the model removes too much energy.
- Aligned processed and original microphone audio across strength edits, on/off transitions and failed model frames. This fixes timing mismatches that could make transitions sound thin or robotic.
- Retuned strength for steady cleanup without fully discarding the original microphone. Zero means off; turning noise removal back on from zero restores Balanced strength. Pending or rejected edits preserve confirmed settings.
- Live processing consistently uses the packaged RNNoise model. Optional model files no longer change the microphone's sound or latency automatically.
- Fixed the Audio status message so a routing failure is not mistaken for a missing VB-CABLE driver.

On reference speech mixed with room noise at 12 dB SNR, strength 85 reduced chopped speech from 1.3% to 0.0%, including quiet input tests. Noise-only tests measured about 13–16 dB reduction at strong settings. These results are specific to the samples; listening in a real call remains separate. RNNoise bypass retains 10 ms of alignment delay, and the active stage reports 20 ms of framing and model latency, before Windows and device buffering.

Local validation includes the native audio suite, 557 JavaScript tests, type/source checks, builds, hidden Electron checks at all three supported window sizes, and three QuadCast 2 input-only start/stop cycles without capture overruns or bypassed frames. Saved microphone, gate, EQ and monitoring preferences remain in place.
