Switchboard 0.9.24 improves audio setup, compact spatial controls, and G502 background behavior.

- Audio setup now shows each driver's purpose, installation status, download progress, and restart guidance. Returning to an unfinished setup refreshes detection without a background poller.
- The Audio workspace explains when Windows needs a restart or app mixing needs VB-CABLE.
- Spatial controls keep head tracking visible in compact windows, with room presets on their own row at 1080 x 720.
- Headset sensor resets and reconnects invalidate calibration from the previous sensor reference.
- Healthy G502 discovery reuses confirmed values instead of repeatedly querying the mouse. Battery, profile, and reconnect notifications update or refresh that state; disconnected controls become unavailable.

Validation: 524 JavaScript tests passed, 9 skipped; Capture.Host and Audio.Host deterministic suites passed. Source checks, type checks, and the production build passed. Hidden native Electron reviews covered audio setup and spatial controls at 1080 x 720, 1420 x 900, and 1920 x 1080, including persistence and error states. Driver installation and tracker availability were simulated in those UI reviews.

Physical LED output, live headset tracking, long-running hardware behavior, and an installed update/relaunch cycle remain unverified for this release.
