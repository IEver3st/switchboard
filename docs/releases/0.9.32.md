Switchboard 0.9.32 remembers your chosen outputs, fixes harsh noise at the end of clips, and adds DeepFilterNet3 as a stronger microphone noise removal option.

- Game, Chat, Media and Microphone now remember the devices you choose. When headphones disconnect, Switchboard uses a temporary fallback without replacing your choice, then switches back automatically when they return. The channel strip shows which device it is waiting for.
- Excluded devices are never used as an automatic fallback, so excluded monitors stay silent while headphones are off.
- Fixed harsh, chopped audio at the end of clips in the clip editor. A track that ended slightly before the video was restarted on every frame; finished tracks now stay paused.
- Saving a clip waits briefly for system, chat and microphone audio to finish their last segment, so saved audio runs to the end of the video. Saves never shorten a clip.
- Microphone → Noise removal → Model adds DeepFilterNet3. It does not mix the raw microphone back in while you talk, so keyboard clicks are not let through during speech. Standard (RNNoise) remains the default.

On reference speech and noise at strength 100, DeepFilterNet3 reduced synthetic keyboard clicks by 11.4 dB while talking and 33.8 dB in pauses, compared with 5.5 dB for Standard, and room noise by 36 dB compared with 16 dB. It chopped 1.0% of clean speech frames at 100, 0.3% at 80 and none at 55, and adds about 20 ms of delay. Three QuadCast 2 input-only start/stop cycles ran with no overruns or bypassed frames. Clicks are synthetic, and live call listening is not yet verified. DeepFilterNet3 does not remove room echo.

The DeepFilterNet3 model weights are not bundled because their redistribution license is unresolved upstream. The option runs only after the model has been acquired locally with `bun run acquire:deepfilternet-model`; otherwise Switchboard uses Standard and shows the reason beside the control.
